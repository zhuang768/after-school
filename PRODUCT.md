# Product context

After School is an English-language, local-only graduation account migration workspace for students. It helps discover likely accounts from exported mail, change login email, export important files, and plan work before institutional access ends. It never claims to transfer account ownership.

Platform: responsive browser PWA. No backend, OAuth, accounts, analytics, CDN assets, or runtime external requests. All personal data stays on the device. User-triggered official links open separately. The user explicitly delegated small product and design choices; this brief supplies the research interview and all design constraints.

Success: import synthetic or personal mail locally, inspect evidence, confirm ownership, complete guidance, retain progress after reload, export calendar/report/backup, and repeat offline. A calm, accessible operating interface is the priority. Never invent verification or testing results.

Pinned constraints: quiet and reassuring; no purple/blue gradient, emoji iconography, or generic uniform card grids. All UI copy in one module. Traditional Chinese engineering reports; English interface and privacy policy. Public deployment and remote repository require final explicit approval.

Assumptions: a sidebar workspace and task-first overview are appropriate for repeat use; warm white, ink green, and lime accents convey calm forward movement. No decorative photography is needed for private mail processing.
